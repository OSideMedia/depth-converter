═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
GUIDANCE — not part of either paste block
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

• Keep the ref in the 3–8 s sweet spot. Adherence degrades past ~8 s and refs over ~10 s
  lose it badly. This kit is {duration:.2f}s.

• One dominant motion signal per clip. A ref carrying a camera move AND an action AND a
  lighting change gets followed at random.

• Weight roughly 70% identity refs / 30% motion ref. Over-weighting the motion ref is the
  usual cause of identity drift.

• Identity images must not contain real photographic faces — Seedance rejects them. Use
  AI-generated or stylized references, or an approved likeness from the Ark asset library.

• Match output length to the ref: request about {suggested_output:g}s. A ref shorter than
  the generation leaves the tail uncovered, and what the model does there is undefined.

• Attach this as a refs-only shot. Go straight to "make video" and skip the create-frame
  step — the adapter never sends references alongside a start frame, so a frame-first shot
  silently drops the depth ref.
