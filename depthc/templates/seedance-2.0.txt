GUIDANCE — not part of either paste block
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Every rule carries its provenance: [measured] = a paid fire this repo recorded
(DOCS/LIVE-FIRE.md) · [community] = hands-on community testing, not vendor-documented ·
[reasoned] = follows from the mechanics, untested.

A [measured] rule is measured ON A MODEL, and the model is part of the finding. Everything
below was measured on seedance-2.0 via Ark. None of it automatically transfers to 2.5 —
see templates/seedance-2.5.txt, where the two that provably do NOT are corrected.

• [measured on seedance-2.0] Fire at 720p or 4K, NEVER 1080p. 1080p is 0/3 — the ref
  ignored or overshot every time, across seeds, ref resolutions and prompt phrasings —
  while every other tier is 4/4. Iterate at 720p, master at 4K. Scope: this is a finding
  about 2.0's native 1080p lane. 2.5 does not currently offer 4K at all and its 1080p is a
  separately-wired lane about which these three failures say nothing.

• [community] Keep the ref in the 3–8 s sweet spot. Adherence degrades past ~8 s and refs
  over ~10 s lose it badly. This kit is {duration:.2f}s.

• [community] One dominant motion signal per clip. A ref carrying a camera move AND an
  action AND a lighting change gets followed at random.

• [community] Weight roughly 70% identity refs / 30% motion ref. Over-weighting the motion
  ref is the usual cause of identity drift.

• [community — confirmed once by a measured rejection] Identity images must not contain
  real photographic faces — Seedance rejects them at input moderation. Use AI-generated or
  stylized references, or an approved likeness from the Ark asset library.

• [reasoned] Match output length to the ref: request about {suggested_output:g}s. A ref
  shorter than the generation leaves the tail uncovered, and what the model does there is
  undefined.

• [measured on seedance-2.0] Attach this as a refs-only shot: go to "make video" and
  skip the create-frame step — on 2.0 the adapter sends no references alongside a start
  frame, so a frame-first shot silently drops the depth ref. Scope: 2.5 INVERTS this — it
  accepts both and demotes the frame to a reference image, so on 2.5 skipping create-frame
  throws away a usable staging reference.

{still_guidance}
