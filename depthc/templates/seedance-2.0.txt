GUIDANCE — not part of either paste block
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Every rule carries its provenance: [measured] = a paid fire this repo recorded
(DOCS/LIVE-FIRE.md) · [community] = hands-on community testing, not vendor-documented ·
[reasoned] = follows from the mechanics, untested.

• [measured] Fire at 720p or 4K, NEVER 1080p. 1080p is 0/3 — the ref ignored or overshot
  every time, across seeds, ref resolutions and prompt phrasings — while every other tier
  is 4/4. Iterate at 720p, master at 4K.

• [community] Keep the ref in the 3–8 s sweet spot. Adherence degrades past ~8 s and refs
  over ~10 s lose it badly. This kit is {duration:.2f}s.

• [community] One dominant motion signal per clip. A ref carrying a camera move AND an
  action AND a lighting change gets followed at random.

• [community] Weight roughly 70% identity refs / 30% motion ref. Over-weighting the motion
  ref is the usual cause of identity drift.

• [community — confirmed once by a measured rejection] Identity images must not contain
  real photographic faces — Seedance rejects them at input moderation. Use AI-generated or
  stylized references, or an approved likeness from the Ark asset library.

• [reasoned] Match output length to the ref: request about {suggested_output:g}s. A ref
  shorter than the generation leaves the tail uncovered, and what the model does there is
  undefined.

• [measured] Attach this as a refs-only shot. Go to "make video" and skip the create-frame
  step — the adapter never sends references alongside a start frame, so a frame-first
  shot silently drops the depth ref.

{still_guidance}
